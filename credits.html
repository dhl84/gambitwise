GambitWise credits

This page names the work of others in GambitWise (GambitWise: Learn Chess and Go): the files, the software, the voice model and the sources of the facts. It also names the tools that we used. It is free to read, with no login.

  • These third-party files keep their own licences. The store terms do not limit your rights under the licences of the third-party material listed here.
  • No file in the app is under a licence that asks for attribution (CC BY, the Open Government Licence, Licence Ouverte or ODC-By).
  • The developer and publisher is dav3studios.

The coach's voice

Every spoken line is a recording that we made with a computer voice: the Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (hexgrad, Apache License 2.0, https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro/blob/main/LICENSE), voice bm_george. No person recorded the lines. The model itself is not in the app.

Fonts

Atkinson Hyperlegible by the Braille Institute of America, also as "Ux Reading" with a plain zero, and Andika by SIL International. Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). The licence text of each font is in the app next to the font file.

Lichess says: "Database exports are released under the Creative Commons CC0 license". CC0 asks for no credit, but we give it. We chose short puzzles with the themes fork, pin, skewer and discovered attack, and our own rules and engine checked each one again. The app shows each puzzle after the first move of the Lichess line.

Our own work

dav3studios wrote the app's own code: the GambitWise scripts, our shared app engine (GDScript), the chess and Go engines and the coach. dav3studios also made the boards, pieces and stones (made in code), the calm room, the coach's own puzzles and mini-games, the texts and the sounds (made by computer program, with no recordings).
