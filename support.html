Getting started

Who is it for? Ages 10 and up, their families and teachers. Meta accounts for children aged 10 to 12 are managed by a parent.

Which headsets? Meta Quest 3.

Do I need the internet? No. The app works offline once it is installed.

Comfort and safety

  • Read Meta's health and safety warnings before you play: https://www.meta.com/legal/quest/health-and-safety-warnings/
  • Play in a clear space with the boundary on.
  • Take regular breaks. Stop at once if anyone feels sick, dizzy or uncomfortable.
  • A grown-up should supervise children while they play.
  • If the view faces the wrong way, hold the Meta button on the right controller to recentre it.

Privacy

The app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no chat, no advertising and no in-app purchases. The privacy policy says what the app keeps on the headset and how a grown-up deletes it.

Found a mistake?

Please email us what you saw and where. We check every report and fix mistakes in an update.
